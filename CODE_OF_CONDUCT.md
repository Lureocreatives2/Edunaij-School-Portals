# Community conduct

Treat contributors and school users respectfully. Welcome questions, explain disagreements constructively and focus reviews on the work.

Harassment, discrimination, threats and sharing private information are unacceptable. Keep examples and screenshots free of real student or staff data.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer. Maintainers may remove harmful content or restrict participation, explain decisions where appropriate and protect reporter privacy. A dedicated contact must be configured before community launch.
